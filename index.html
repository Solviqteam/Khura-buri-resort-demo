Our story[MACHINE TRANSLATE]

A family hotel in Khura Buri

Kuraburi Greenview Resort was shaped by a simple idea: that time in Phang Nga should feel part of the landscape, not apart from it. Founded in 1999 and set among tropical rainforest, open greenery and a quiet lake, it offers rooms and bungalows of natural wood, stone and bamboo that give the resort its calm, rustic character.

Days here move gently. Paddle across the lake by kayak, swim, sit down to Thai and Western dishes, or set out for the Surin Islands and the wider Phang Nga coast. However you spend them, the aim is the same: a restful stay that lets you enjoy the natural beauty of the region.

Kuraburi Greenview Resort is a family hotel in Khura Buri District, Phang Nga, Thailand, with 39 rooms and bungalows. The resort is preparing to reopen.
